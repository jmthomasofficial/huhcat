A trader sells for one reason above all others: they cannot see what happens next. The launch is loud. Then the feed goes quiet. Then the deployer's next coin appears. Most projects never build it because building it means showing up daily with no launch-day dopamine. That is the whole opening for a community that already proved it stays.
